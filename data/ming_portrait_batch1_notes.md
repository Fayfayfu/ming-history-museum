# 明史人物画像第一批

## 选择规则

第一批兼顾人物历史评分、跨领域影响、公众辨识度、人物馆当前曝光和画像缺口。画像按证据性质分为明代画像、清初绘像、近代纪念性绘像、小说插图和 AI 想象像；这些标签描述图像来源，不等同于人物真实容貌置信度。

本批人物：徐达、李时珍、徐霞客、王守仁、徐光启、郑和、沈周、唐寅、仇英、刘基、常遇春、秦良玉。

## 秦良玉 AI 想象像

生成方式：Codex 内置 `image_gen`。

最终提示词：

> Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: vertical portrait for a Chinese Ming-history digital museum. Create a historically grounded AI imagined portrait of Qin Liangyu around age 55 during the 1630 defense mobilization. The Ming History describes her as courageous and intelligent, skilled at riding and archery, literate, composed and elegant in bearing, but strict in command; her troops were known as the White Pole Soldiers. A surviving robe associated with her at the China Three Gorges Museum is a red silk garment with gold dragon-and-phoenix embroidery. Show a mature Chinese woman commander with a weathered but composed face, upright posture and disciplined gaze. Use historically plausible late-Ming high-ranking command attire, a restrained deep-red robe with subtle gold embroidery and practical dark inner layers. Place her against muted aged silk with a military map, southwest mountains and faint pale wooden spear shafts. Painterly museum realism, warm umber and mineral pigments, vertical waist-up composition. No text, watermark, fantasy armor, sexualization, youthful glamour, Qing clothing, Japanese or European armor.

史实依据：

- 《明史·秦良玉传》所载性格、骑射、文翰、仪度、军纪与白杆兵。
- 重庆中国三峡博物馆相关页面对秦良玉平金绣龙凤袍的说明。

边界：面貌、发式细节、神态、地图和空间组合没有同时代肖像证据，均属于艺术想象。
